## How this scan was done, and how far to trust it

The cloud sandbox this ran in only allows package registries and GitHub, so the booking
sites (Google Flights, Skyscanner, Kayak, BA, Virgin, AA, JetBlue) could not be opened
directly. The scan went through **web search** of those sites' indexed pages instead:
- the "from £X" fares that airlines and Kayak publish for sample date pairs, which Virgin
  says were collected within the previous 48 hours;
- schedule data from FlightMapper, AeroRoutes and the airlines.

What that means:

- A fare below is **evidence that a price level exists, not a quote you can book.** Expect
  it to have moved by a few pounds either way.
- Coverage is patchy. **Most grid cells are empty because no sample was published for those
  dates, not because there are no flights.**
- "From" fares are usually the most basic economy, often hand luggage only. A checked bag
  and seat selection usually cost extra.
- Flight times come from schedule sites seen through search, with the 1 November US clock
  change applied. Confirm times when booking.

## Things that affect cost and how pleasant the trip is

- **Sun 1 Nov — New York City Marathon**
  ([newyork.co.uk](https://www.newyork.co.uk/new-york-marathon/)). Over 50,000 runners,
  many from the UK.
  - Flights out on 29–31 Oct and back on 2–3 Nov are busier.
  - Manhattan hotels are at peak price that weekend.
  - Flying out **Mon 2 – Wed 4 Nov** avoids the crush. Skyscanner currently reports Mon 2 Nov
    as the cheapest day to fly LHR→JFK
    ([Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.net/routes/lhr/jfk/london-heathrow-to-new-york-john-f-kennedy.html)).
- **Clocks.** UK clocks go back on 25 Oct and US clocks on 1 Nov. For 26–31 Oct, New York is
  only 4 hours behind (5 hours from 1 Nov), which shifts flight times by an hour that week.
- **Thu 26 Nov — Thanksgiving; Fri 27 Nov — Black Friday.** Hotels are dear and shops packed
  over the holiday weekend. Sun 29 Nov is one of the busiest days of the year at the New York
  airports. Flying out on Mon 30 Nov or Tue 1 Dec misses the worst of it.
- **Wed 2 Dec — Rockefeller Center tree lighting**
  ([newyork.co.uk](https://www.newyork.co.uk/rockefeller-center-christmas-tree-lighting-ceremony/)).
  It falls inside the December window and is fun to see, but Midtown is packed that evening.
- **Day of week for day flights back.** JetBlue's morning flight back runs only Mon/Fri/Sun.
  BA178, VS26 and United's flights run daily.
- **Buying through Finnair or Iberia.** The £385 and £376 fares are partner airlines selling
  seats on BA or American flights. That's legitimate and often cheaper. Seat selection,
  changes and upgrades are then handled by the airline you bought from, which can be clunkier.

## Recommendation

1. **If you lean towards December:**
   - **Out Tue 1 Dec, back Sun 6 Dec** (around £385) is the cheapest valid fare found.
   - Try to get BA117 out (08:20) and BA178 back (08:05). The partner fare may not let you
     pick those exact flights, so check the times before paying.
2. **If you lean towards November:**
   - **Out Tue 3 Nov, back Sat 7 – Tue 10 Nov** (around £406 with American).
   - Or pay a little more for **Virgin VS3/VS26** on the same dates. It's the nicest aircraft
     and morning flights both ways.
3. **Either way:** book Heathrow, morning out, day flight back. In basic economy, BA, Virgin
   and American are within about £50 of each other on these dates, so pick on times and
   aircraft, not price.
